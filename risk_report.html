Risk Calculations
Parametric VaRNormal distribution, 5-year daily volatility, square-root horizon scaling.
EWMA VaRRiskMetrics lambda=0.94, more sensitive to recent moves.
Historical VaROverlapping 30-day windows from empirical returns.
Monte Carlo10,000 correlated GBM paths, zero-drift VaR convention.
Mark-to-MarketReal matched market price per grade where available; unpriced modelled rows are held at book unless futures fallback is explicitly enabled.
Net Realizable ValueGross MTM less metal-specific operating haircuts for freight, handling, shrink, and bid/ask. Liquidation value applies wider forced-sale haircuts.
Risk ExposureVaR, Monte Carlo, and stress scenarios use net realizable saleable exposure. Unpriced rows are held at book for MTM but included in risk through a conservative proxy.
Basis StressBasis compression applies independent percentage-point reductions to scrap basis, capped at each row's current basis.
Valuation QualityA = real matched sale price. Unpriced = no usable market price, held at book, zero unrealized gain and zero price sensitivity.
Model Assumptions
AssumptionValueSourceConfidenceReviewedRationale
Scrap Basis Vol Multiplier 1.25 management/model overlay low 2026-06-16 Scrap prices can move more sharply than exchange futures when basis widens in selloffs.
Net Realizable Haircuts copper=4%, aluminium=6%, steel=8%, stainless=7%, brass=5%, lead=12%, zinc=10% management estimate medium-low 2026-06-16 Approximates freight, handling, shrink, bid/ask, and normal sale friction.
Liquidation Haircuts copper=10%, aluminium=15%, steel=20%, stainless=18%, brass=12%, lead=30%, zinc=25% management estimate medium-low 2026-06-16 Approximates forced-sale discount by metal.
Proxy Metals stainless->copper, brass->copper, lead->copper, zinc->copper free price-data availability low 2026-06-16 Used where no reliable free exchange ticker is available for the scrap category.
Stress Scenarios 2008 Financial Crisis, 2015 China Slowdown, 2022 Rate Shock, Demand Collapse (-40%), 2021 Supercycle Rally model-defined historical/hypothetical shocks medium-low 2026-06-16 Applies deterministic commodity shocks to current risk exposure.
Unpriced Inventory Risk Proxy max(net realizable book value, futures-basis proxy value) conservative model policy medium 2026-06-16 Unpriced rows are held at book for MTM but still included in risk exposure using the higher conservative risk value.
